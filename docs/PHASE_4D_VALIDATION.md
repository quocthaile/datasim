## 5. Negative & Fail-Closed Trust-Boundary Coverage

All 11 negative and deterministic categories in `run_pdf_to_simulation_gate` passed (`768` total assertions):

1. **N-01 (Incorrect exact MPN / publisher / suffix)**: Mismatched MPN (`SIM-R-10K-WRONG`), undeclared publisher mismatch, and `part_number_suffix` mismatch (`TR`) fail closed without producing a candidate bundle.
2. **N-02 (Invalid PDF page index)**: Out-of-range `pdf_page_index = 2` on the 2-page PDF and out-of-range `pdf_page_index = 5` in `IntakeManifest` fail closed.
3. **N-03 (Missing selectable PDF text)**: `image_only_datasheet.pdf` and an empty coordinate band fail closed without OCR fallback.
4. **N-04 (Ambiguous numeric values)**: `"1,000"`, `"1.0*"`, and blank-unit Row 4 remain unparsed (`parsed_numeric_value == std::nullopt`), asserting a scalar on `"1,000"` fails `validate_intake_manifest` (`asserted_ambiguous_numeric_value`), and missing finite SI values fail `prepare_vertical_rc_simulation` (`missing_or_invalid_si_value`).
5. **N-05 (Conflicting observations without documented resolution)**: Approving both `25 degC` competing resistor observations without a `ParameterConflict` record fails `validate_domain_bundle` (`undeclared_parameter_conflict`), leaving a conflict `unresolved` fails `prepare_vertical_rc_simulation` (`unresolved_knowledge_conflicts`), and calling `promote_parameter_to_knowledge` on an observation in an unresolved conflict throws `std::invalid_argument`.
6. **N-06 (Candidate promoted without required review transitions)**: Direct `candidate -> approved` transitions and `promote_parameter_to_knowledge` on `candidate` or `needs_review` observations throw `std::invalid_argument`; unreviewed candidate observations in an assembly fail `prepare_vertical_rc_simulation` (`unapproved_source_observation`).
7. **N-07 (Approval attempted by `automatic_parser` / `llm_assistant` / `automated_check`)**: Non-human approval attempts throw `std::invalid_argument` and fail domain/vertical validation (`unauthorized_reviewer_kind`).
8. **N-08 (Missing / incompatible model validation & policy gate)**: `unvalidated` (`model_unvalidated`), `failed_validation` (`model_failed_validation`), missing `transient` in `verified_analyses` (`analysis_not_verified_in_validation_record`), and `ExecutionPolicy::engineering_approved_only` on synthetic PDF fixtures (`synthetic_data_prohibited_in_engineering_mode`) all fail closed.
9. **N-09 (Operating conditions outside validity envelope)**: Runtime `ambient_temperature_degC = 150.0`, runtime `stimulus_voltage_V = 60.0`, and parameter `T_A = 85 degC` against a `[ -20, 70 ] degC` model envelope all fail closed (`operating_condition_outside_validity_envelope`).
10. **N-10 (Missing Poppler or `ngspice` executable)**: Missing `pdfinfo`, missing `pdftotext`, and missing `ngspice` fail closed without producing approved models or simulation outputs.
11. **Determinism**: Repeat PDF extraction, repeat `CircuitAssembly` construction, and repeat `ngspice` execution produce identical structs and byte-identical output files.

## 6. Observed Limitations & Remaining Risks

- **Synthetic Fixture Scope Only**: All Phase 4D evidence is derived from fictional, locally generated PDFs (`synthetic_datasheet.pdf` and `image_only_datasheet.pdf`) and synthetic review/model fixtures (`ExecutionClassification::test_only_synthetic`). No real manufacturer datasheet accuracy or physical hardware measurement (`MEASURED`) is claimed.
- **Explicit Column Geometry Required**: `datasim_pdf` extracts only explicitly configured horizontal coordinate spans from text-selectable PDFs; multi-line wrapped cells, rotated tables, graphic curves, and scanned/image-only pages are rejected.
- **Ideal Linear Passive RC Backend Only**: Simulation remains scoped to first-order linear passive RC circuits on the existing `datasim` / `datasim_vertical` adapter.
